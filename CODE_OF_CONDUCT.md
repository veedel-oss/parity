# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in Parity, in issues, pull requests, reviews and every other project space, is treated with respect. This code applies to all of them, maintainers included, and also when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Expected

- Be friendly and patient, and assume good intent.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Stay on topic and respect other people's time.
- When you decline a proposal, say why. When yours is declined, accept it.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks and threats.
- Remarks about a person's background, identity, body or abilities.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission. This includes host names, addresses and configuration from their systems, which bug reports sometimes contain.
- Trolling, spam, and carrying on after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Write to veedel-oss@mailbox.org. Only the maintainers read the reports, and they keep them confidential. Links or screenshots help.

## Consequences

The maintainers decide what happens: a reminder, the removal of comments, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project. Where they can, they explain their decision to the people involved.
